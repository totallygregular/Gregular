WELCOME, TRAVELLER

A town that remembers.

Walk between five places in one small town. Accept a notice in the square, investigate objects and conversations, and choose how to resolve each of three connected jobs. Read the consequence of your action before moving on.

You can take the jobs in any order. Some decisions affect later dialogue, rewards, objects and routes. Optional items are never required to finish. Injuries can be healed without payment. When the jobs are finished, attend the town meeting, revisit the locals, make amends, or stay for another morning.

This is an authored branching story with a definite ending and revisitable aftermath. It has no AI narrator, free-text actions, account, server, analytics or audio. Town artwork was generated for this prototype; all story and mechanics are scripted.

Saves are stored only in your browser. The published version would use GitHub Pages, whose privacy statement covers hosting request information.

Use Tab and Enter to choose actions. Escape closes a dialog. Nothing is timed.
