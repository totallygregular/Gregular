    FATE, WITH A FEW RULES

    How to draft.

    1. Bring your party. Enter unique player names and choose tank, healer or DPS. Unassigned players count as DPS.
    2. Roll the d20s. Tanks pick first, healers second, DPS last. Higher rolls go first inside each role. Reroll before locking if you like.
    3. Break ties with a tournament. The app throws random paper, scissors or rock. Draws repeat. A fresh random bracket decides each place, so every tied player gets a distinct rank.
    4. Spin for a class. Every displayed slice has the same chance. Picks come out of the shared pool. Some eligible classes may be held back to keep later role assignments possible; the wheel explains those exclusions.
    5. Share the damage. Copy the results. New draft keeps your names and class pools, clears rolls and picks, and starts again.

    This is a shared-screen tool. Saves stay in this browser; there is no online lobby or cross-device sync. Reloading during a spin or tournament recovers the same already-selected result. The app doesn’t connect to WoW.
